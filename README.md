# Patric Tengelin — Global Mobility & International Tax Research

Independent researcher and writer focused on global mobility, residency frameworks, and international tax systems affecting location-independent professionals.

Over the past decade, I have lived and worked across more than fifteen countries, studying how jurisdictions structure taxation, residency, and incentives for globally mobile individuals.

## Areas of Focus

- Global mobility
- Residency frameworks
- International taxation
- Remote work systems
- Cross-border regulatory environments
